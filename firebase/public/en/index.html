FAQ

How much is it?

A one-time purchase. No ads and no in-app purchases.

Do I need to sign in?

No. You can tap right after the first launch. Your sets are kept in an account the app creates on first launch.

What happens when I switch iPhones?

Sign in with Apple from Settings, then sign in with the same Apple ID on the new iPhone and your sets come back.

How many squats?

20 to start. You can change it from 5 to 100 in Settings.

Does it need a connection?

Only on the first launch. After that, you can tap offline.

Which devices?

iPhone with iOS 26 or later.
