Yat Tat Ta is an iPhone app for after-meal squats. Tap the button once per squat to turn it into Dopa and log the set.

Contactsupport@yat-tat-ta.com Privacy PolicyWhat we collect, and how it is used

Your sets and account

  • No sign-in needed. Your sets are kept in an account the app creates on first launch.
  • Sign in with Apple from Settings, and your sets come back on a new iPhone when you sign in with the same Apple ID.
  • You can delete all your sets and your account with "Delete Account" (or "Delete All Records" if you have not signed in) in the app's settings.
