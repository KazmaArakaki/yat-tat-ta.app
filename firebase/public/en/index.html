Yat Tat Ta is an iPhone app for after-meal squats. Tap the button once per squat to turn it into Dopa and log the set.

Your sets and account

  • Your sets stay with the account you sign in to with Apple. On a new iPhone, sign in with the same Apple ID to get them back.
  • You can delete all your sets and your account with "Delete Account" in the app's settings.
