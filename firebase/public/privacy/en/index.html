Privacy Policy

Effective: October 7, 2026

Revised: October 8, 2026

  • We collect only your account ID and your squat sets. You can use the App without signing in. We never receive your name or email.
  • Nothing is used for ads, analytics, or tracking, and no other user can see it.
  • You can delete all your sets and your account at any time in the app's settings.

This policy explains how Kazuma Arakaki ("we") handles your information in the iPhone app Yat Tat Ta (the "App").

1What we collect

InformationDetails
Account IDThe user ID that Firebase Authentication assigns when you first open the App. You do not enter anything for it, and it is not linked to information that identifies you. Only if you sign in with Apple do we also receive a sign-in identifier from Apple. We do not ask Apple for your name or email, and we never receive them.
Squat setsFor each set: when you started tapping, the day it counts for, the number of squats, the Dopa earned in Fever Time, and when our server received it.
Connection dataWhen the App talks to Firebase, technical data such as your IP address and device type may be processed on Google's servers. We do not collect or use this data per user.

Information that stays on your iPhone

Your squats per set, your Not-Yet Reminder settings, and markers for notices you've already seen stay on your iPhone and are never sent anywhere. Reminders are also scheduled on your iPhone.

2How we use it

  • To save your squat sets and show the same sets when you switch phones or reinstall the App.
  • To keep your account, and to make sure only you can read and write your sets.
  • To prevent abuse and keep the App running safely.

We use it for nothing else. It is never used to show ads, analyze usage, or track you across other apps and websites.

3Where it is stored

We store your information with Firebase, a service of Google LLC (United States).

  • Cloud Firestore stores your squat sets in Tokyo (asia-northeast1).
  • Firebase Authentication manages your account ID. It may be processed in Google data centers, including outside Japan.

Google describes how it protects data held in Firebase in its data processing terms.

Sign in with Apple is optional and provided by Apple. Apple handles your information under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Apart from the above, we never share your information with third parties without your consent, unless required by law.

4How long we keep it, and how to delete it

We keep your information only while your account exists.

Tap "Delete Account" in the App's settings to delete all your sets and your account and to disconnect the App from your Apple ID. If you have not signed in with Apple, tap "Delete All Records" to delete all your sets. The emptied anonymous account is deleted, or, if it cannot be deleted, the App stops using it and it remains without any sets attached. Deleted information cannot be restored. It may take some time before it is fully removed from Google's systems.

Signing out does not delete your sets. Sign in again with the same Apple ID to get them back. After you sign out, the App starts over with a new account and no sets.

5Security

All communication between the App and Firebase is encrypted. Your saved sets can be read and written only from the account that created them.

6Your requests

To access, correct, stop the use of, or delete your information, contact us at the address below. We will confirm your identity and respond as required by law. You can also delete everything right away with "Delete Account" (or "Delete All Records" if you have not signed in) in the App.

7Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the update on this page and add the revision date below the effective date.
